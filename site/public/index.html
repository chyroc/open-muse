A personal AI agent that gets things done

It remembers, follows up, and works for you — on your iPhone or Android phone, on your Mac, and on a cloud computer that keeps going after you close the app.

Join the iPhone beta Download for Mac Download for Android Source on GitHub

Free, with the source on GitHub. Needs a Volcano Ark API key with Managed Agents.

What it can do

One assistant across your devices. Conversations, memory, goals, and reminders follow your Open Muse account.

Your health, on demand

Ask “how did I sleep this week?” and it reads Apple Health on your iPhone, or Health Connect on Android, right then — steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, weight. It never makes up a number it could not read.

Your Mac, hands-on

With your permission it looks at the screen, opens apps and files, clicks, types, and scrolls — in any app. Every action waits for your approval unless you allow it ahead of time.

Lark (Feishu), end to end

Sign in once, then ask in plain language: brief me before my next meeting, turn today's group chat into tasks, write up yesterday's meeting notes.

A cloud computer that keeps working

Each workspace has its own sandbox with web search, a real browser, a terminal, and files. Hand over a task, close the app, and the result is waiting when you come back.

Remembers and follows up

Keeps what matters in memory you can read and edit, turns goals into plans and checks in on them, and reminds you once, daily, weekly, or monthly.

A Feed and Ideas of your own

Morning updates on the topics you choose, each with a source, and ready-made tasks drawn from your goals and recent conversations.

Built to be trusted

Asks first

Using your Mac or reading your health data waits for your OK unless you allow it ahead of time. Calendar and location ask every time.

No pretend answers

Without a working connection the app says so. It never generates a simulated reply.

Your key, your workspace

The apps call Volcano Ark directly with your own API key, stored encrypted for your account. Accounts sharing a key still get separate workspaces and memory.

Memory you can see

Your assistant's name, persona, and remembered facts are plain documents you can open, edit, or clear.

Get started

iPhone

Join the public beta on TestFlight, register with an email and password, then connect your Volcano Ark API key in Settings.

Android

Download the app (version {{android.version}}, {{android.size}}) and open it to install; Android asks you to allow installs from your browser the first time. It needs Android 9 or later, and Android 14 or later for Health Connect. The download comes from a server near you; you can also pick mainland China or global.

SHA-256 {{android.sha256}}

Mac

Download the app (version {{macos.version}}, {{macos.size}}), open the disk image, and drag Open Muse to Applications. It is signed and notarized by Apple, and runs on macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The download comes from a server near you; you can also pick mainland China or global.

SHA-256 {{macos.sha256}}

Web

Build it from the source on GitHub; the development guide covers it.

What it costs

The apps are free. Model and cloud usage is billed to the Volcano Engine account that owns your API key.
