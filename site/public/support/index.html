Open Muse Privacy Support 中文

Support

Email support@getopenmuse.com. In the app, Settings > Report a problem (or shaking your phone) prepares a report you can send by email or any other app; in TestFlight you can also send feedback with a screenshot.

Getting started

  1. Install Open Muse from TestFlight on iPhone, or download it for Android, and open it.
  2. Register or sign in with an email address and password. No email confirmation is needed, and there is no password reset in the app, so keep the password.
  3. Connect a Volcano Ark API key with access to Managed Agents in Settings > Account and workspace. If you were given an account with a key already connected, skip this step.
  4. Start chatting. The first conversation sets up your assistant, its cloud environment, and its memory.

Common questions

Does it cost anything? The app is free. Model and cloud usage is billed to the Volcano Engine account that owns the API key.

Why can't the assistant read my Health data? Allow access when iOS asks, or turn it on in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Open Muse. On Android, allow it when Health Connect asks, or in Settings > Security & privacy > Privacy > Health Connect > App permissions > Open Muse.

Android says the app can't be installed. Allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. An installed copy signed differently, such as a development build, must be uninstalled first.

How do I delete my data? See the privacy policy.

支持

发邮件到 support@getopenmuse.com。在 App 里，「设置 > 报告问题」（或摇一摇手机）会生成一份报告，你可以用邮件或其他 App 发送；也可以在 TestFlight 里附截图发送反馈。

开始使用

  1. 在 iPhone 上从 TestFlight 安装，或下载 Android 版，然后打开 Open Muse。
  2. 用邮箱和密码注册或登录。不需要邮箱验证；App 里没有找回密码，请记好密码。
  3. 在「设置 > 账号与工作区」连接一个能使用 Managed Agents 的火山方舟 API Key。如果你拿到的账号已经连好了 Key，跳过这一步。
  4. 开始聊天。第一次对话会准备好你的助手、云端环境和记忆。

常见问题

要付费吗？App 免费。模型和云端用量计入 API Key 所属的火山引擎账号。

助手为什么读不到我的健康数据？在 iOS 询问时允许访问，或在 iOS「设置 > 健康 > 数据访问与设备 > Open Muse」里打开。Android 上，在 Health Connect 询问时允许，或在「设置 > 安全和隐私 > 隐私 > Health Connect > 应用权限 > Open Muse」里打开。

Android 提示无法安装？在 Android 询问时，允许浏览器或文件管理器安装未知应用。如果手机上已经装了签名不同的版本（例如开发版），需要先卸载。

怎么删除我的数据？见隐私政策。
