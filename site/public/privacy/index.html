Open Muse Privacy Support 中文

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Open Muse is a personal AI assistant. This policy explains what the Open Muse app and the Open Muse service handle, where it goes, and how to remove it. Questions: support@getopenmuse.com.

Your Open Muse account

You sign in with an email address and password. The account lets the Open Muse service keep your settings separate from everyone else's. The Open Muse service runs on Volcano Engine (Volcengine) cloud infrastructure in mainland China and stores:

  • your email address and a password hash, kept by the sign-in provider;
  • the Volcano Ark API key you connect, encrypted;
  • the IDs of the assistant, cloud environment, and memory store created for you, encrypted;
  • an encrypted copy of your main chat, saved replies, and Feed and Ideas posts, so your devices stay in sync;
  • your check-in schedule and time zone, and the reminders you set;
  • if you connect Lark (Feishu), the Lark sign-in tokens, encrypted;
  • a list of your devices with their platform, app version, and when each was last seen.

Conversations and the AI model

What you write, the files and photos you attach, and the device data you choose to share are sent to Volcano Ark (Volcano Engine's model and Managed Agents service) to produce replies. The app calls Ark directly with the API key connected to your account, and Ark keeps your conversations, the assistant's memory, and the work done in your cloud environment under that key's Volcano Engine account. Volcano Engine processes this data under its own privacy policy and terms.

If someone else gave you the API key you use (for example during a beta test), your conversations and memory are stored in their Volcano Engine account and they can access them there.

Apple Health and other device data

Open Muse reads Apple Health only after you allow it in iOS, and only when the assistant needs a figure to answer you, such as your steps, workouts, sleep, heart rate, or weight. Until you connect Apple Health in the app, each read asks you first. Calendar, Reminders, and Contacts are read only after you approve each request in the chat. The data read is sent to Ark as part of that conversation, as described above. Open Muse never writes to Apple Health, never uses Health data for advertising or marketing, and never sells or shares it with data brokers.

The camera and microphone are used only when you take a photo or record a video to send.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs, and no crash reporting service.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties other than the services described here.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings > Account and workspace > Export my data saves a copy of everything the Open Muse service keeps for your account.
  • Delete: Settings > Account and workspace > Delete account removes your account and everything the Open Muse service keeps for it. Conversations and memory stored in Ark stay in the Volcano Engine account that owns the API key and can be deleted there.
  • Health access: Disconnect Apple Health in Connectors, or change access in iOS Settings > Health.

Children

Open Muse is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page when these practices change and revise the date above.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 7 日

Open Muse 是一个个人 AI 助手。本政策说明 Open Muse App 和 Open Muse 服务会处理哪些数据、这些数据去了哪里，以及如何删除。有问题请联系 support@getopenmuse.com。

Open Muse 账号

你用邮箱和密码登录。账号让 Open Muse 服务把你的设置和其他人分开保存。Open Muse 服务运行在中国大陆的火山引擎云上，保存以下内容：

  • 你的邮箱地址和密码哈希，由登录服务保存；
  • 你连接的火山方舟 API Key，加密保存；
  • 为你创建的助手、云端环境和记忆库的 ID，加密保存；
  • 主对话、收藏的回复、动态和点子的加密副本，用于在你的设备间同步；
  • 你的定时跟进时间、时区，以及你设置的提醒；
  • 如果你连接了飞书，飞书的登录令牌，加密保存；
  • 你的设备列表，包括平台、App 版本和最近在线时间。

对话与 AI 模型

你写的内容、附带的文件和照片，以及你同意分享的设备数据，会发送给火山方舟（火山引擎的模型与 Managed Agents 服务）来生成回复。App 用你账号里连接的 API Key 直接调用方舟；对话、助手的记忆和云端环境里完成的工作，都保存在这把 Key 所属的火山引擎账号下。火山引擎按其隐私政策和服务条款处理这些数据。

如果你用的 API Key 是别人提供的（例如参加测试时），你的对话和记忆会保存在对方的火山引擎账号里，对方可以在那里看到。

Apple 健康和其他设备数据

只有在你于 iOS 中允许后，Open Muse 才会读取 Apple 健康，而且只在助手需要某个数字来回答你时读取，例如步数、体能训练、睡眠、心率或体重。在 App 里连接 Apple 健康之前，每次读取都会先问你。日历、提醒事项和通讯录只有在你于对话中批准每次请求后才会读取。读到的数据会作为对话的一部分发送给方舟，处理方式见上文。Open Muse 从不写入 Apple 健康，从不把健康数据用于广告或营销，也从不出售或分享给数据经纪商。

相机和麦克风只在你拍照或录像发送时使用。

我们不做的事

  • 不含广告、追踪或统计 SDK，也不使用崩溃上报服务。
  • 除本文所述的服务外，不出售你的数据，也不与第三方分享。

你的选择

  • 导出：「设置 > 账号与工作区 > 导出我的数据」会保存 Open Muse 服务为你的账号保存的全部内容。
  • 删除：「设置 > 账号与工作区 > 删除账号」会删除你的账号，以及 Open Muse 服务为它保存的全部内容。保存在方舟里的对话和记忆仍在 API Key 所属的火山引擎账号下，可以在那里删除。
  • 健康数据访问：在「连接器」里断开 Apple 健康，或在 iOS「设置 > 健康」里修改权限。

儿童

Open Muse 不面向 13 岁以下的儿童。
